CAGE LEGENDS — KO / CAREER / ROUND SCORING UPDATE

- Knockdowns now start only after significant regional health loss: jab uses a 40% damage threshold, other strikes 30%.
- Knockdown probability rises linearly and is capped at 60% by 90% regional damage. Attack types add small weighting but cannot exceed 60%.
- Added saved-fighter dropdown and W-L-D career ranking using browser localStorage on the host device.
- Fight records update once on decision, KO, or submission end.
- Automatic round scoring compares remaining head/body health plus a small stamina component. Final decision uses round wins after three 3-minute rounds.
- Submission fighters are drawn in a grounded pose during the submission and play the get-up animation when escaping.

Persistence note: career records are stored in localStorage on the host browser/device, not shared across different host devices/browsers. Shared cross-device leaderboards require a Supabase database table and policies.
- JavaScript syntax checked with Node.js. Not tested in a live two-controller fight session.
