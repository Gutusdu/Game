CAGE LEGENDS — Octagon Movement + AI Difficulty Update

Changes:
- Fighter movement is clamped to the playable octagon floor polygon rather than the rectangular canvas bounds. This applies to both fighters and both human players.
- Added five AI difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, Ultimate, Impossible.
- Difficulty affects AI movement speed, attack cadence/aggression, attack accuracy/damage, and defensive reactions.
- Impossible is intended to be extremely difficult but not mathematically unbeatable; AI decisions still include randomness and the player can still win.
- AI fights do not create or update career wins/losses/draws and do not affect the ranking. Human-vs-human results continue to count.

Validation performed:
- `node --check host.js`
- Verified all five difficulty options and octagon clamping are present.
- No live browser or two-phone multiplayer test was performed.
