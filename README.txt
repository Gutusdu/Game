CAGE LEGENDS ARCADE V4 — BALANCE / CRITICALS
- Damage reduced substantially so jab spam is not enough to rapidly drain health.
- Critical chance depends on attack type, target health, and both fighters' stamina.
- Crit chance rises sharply as target health gets low; at full health it remains extremely rare, but rare critical flash-KOs are possible.
- Critical hits deal 2.25x damage and have stronger hit particles.
- Tired attackers have higher critical chance; tired defenders are more vulnerable.
- Kicks cost 34 stamina, so normally two consecutive kicks require a short recovery.
- Stamina recovers faster while idle; max stamina gradually wears down with repeated attacks.
